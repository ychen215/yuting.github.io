Welcome

Small area estimation Survey statistics Uncertainty quantification

I am an Assistant Professor of Statistics in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Eastern Kentucky University. I received my Ph.D. in Survey and Data Science from the University of Maryland, College Park, where I worked with Partha Lahiri.

My research develops robust statistical methods for producing reliable local estimates when direct samples are small, models may be imperfect, and uncertainty must be communicated clearly. Current applications include official statistics, poverty mapping, and survey data integration.

Research interests

  • Small area estimation
  • Survey statistics and data integration
  • Mixed-effects models and robust inference
  • Prediction intervals and uncertainty quantification
  • Poverty indicators and official statistics
